# Aromatic Oils Dashboard

This Power BI project analyzes sales and pricing data for an online shop selling aromatic oils.  
The dataset contains 10 products, individual prices between 5-10 euros, for a full 12 months.  

**Skills demonstrated:** Power BI, Data visualization, KPIs, DAX, Trends, Simple calculations.


# Business Questions Answered

What is the total revenue and profit?

How do sales and profit change over time?

Which products generate the highest profit?

Which months perform best and worst?

How does price affect profitability?

# Dataset

Synthetic sales data (12 months)

Product-based sales information

Pricing, cost, revenue, and profit metrics

Main Columns:

Month

Product

Units Sold

Price (EUR)

Revenue (EUR)

Cost (EUR)

Profit (EUR)

# Dashboard Structure

**Page 1 – Sales Overview**

KPI Cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Units Sold, Profit Margin

Line Chart: Revenue trend by month

Bar Chart: Profit by product

Table: Detailed sales data

Product slicer for filtering

**Page 2 – Advanced Analysis**

Scatter Plot: Price vs Profit by product

Profit Margin analysis by product

Best and worst performing months

Interactive filters for deeper exploration
